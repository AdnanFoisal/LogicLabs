Logic Labs is a comprehensive, 100% offline digital-logic laboratory: a faithful digital twin of the K&H IDL-800A Digital Lab Trainer, featuring a Tinkercad-inspired 2.5D breadboard, datasheet-accurate 74xx TTL integrated circuits, and a discrete-event simulation engine derived from hneemann/Digital.

Version: 1.0.4.4.4

THE BREADBOARD (AD-200)
• True AD-200 topology: 1,896 tie-points across four 64-column terminal blocks, seven distribution power rails, and the central DIP IC trench.
• Interactive hand wiring: drag-to-wire or tap-to-connect jumpers with magnetic socket snapping.
• 2.2x magnifier loupe for pinpoint wiring accuracy on small screens.
• 10 insulation wire colors with realistic 2.5D sag geometry.
• Wire management: select, delete, recolor, or inspect individual nets.

DATASHEET-EXACT 74xx TTL ICs
Place, rotate, and wire 14 real-world TTL integrated circuits:
• 7400: Quad 2-input NAND gates
• 7402: Quad 2-input NOR gates
• 7404: Hex Inverters (NOT gates)
• 7408: Quad 2-input AND gates
• 7410: Triple 3-input NAND gates
• 7411: Triple 3-input AND gates
• 7420: Dual 4-input NAND gates
• 7432: Quad 2-input OR gates
• 7448: BCD to 7-Segment Decoder
• 7474: Dual D-Type Edge-Triggered Flip-Flops (Preset/Clear)
• 7476: Dual J-K Flip-Flops (Preset/Clear)
• 7483: 4-Bit Binary Full Adder with Fast Carry
• 7486: Quad 2-input XOR gates
• 74266: Quad 2-input XNOR gates (Open-Collector)
• Complete in-app pinout diagrams and functional truth tables for every IC.

THE TRAINER CONSOLE (IDL-800A)
• 8 SPDT toggle logic switches (SW0–SW7) with mechanical action.
• 2 debounced momentary pulsers with complementary Q and Q_bar outputs.
• 8 buffered LED monitors featuring persistence-of-vision and optical decay.
• Stepped clock generator: 0.5 Hz, 1 Hz, 10 Hz, 100 Hz, 1 kHz, 10 kHz, and 100 kHz.
• Dual BCD-driven 7-segment LED display modules.
• Master bench power switch with VCC and GND rail control.
• Two simulation modes: STANDARD (safe learning) and PRACTICAL (realistic reverse-polarity blowout and bus-contention burnout).

DIAGNOSTIC INSTRUMENTS
• Real-time 2-channel Oscilloscope: tap any breadboard tie-point or console pin to probe signals with adjustable timebase, voltage scale, and edge triggering.
• Boolean Diagram View: automatically renders the gate-level schematic of your breadboard circuit with live net coloring and boolean expressions.

ACADEMIC COURSEWORK & VERIFICATION
• 5 Classic University Labs: Basic Gates, Universal NAND/NOR Realization, Binary Adders, Multiplexers/Decoders, and Sequential Flip-Flops.
• 13 Extended Experiments: Gray code converters, magnitude comparators, BCD arithmetic, shift registers, and weighted-resistor DACs.
• One-tap Truth-Table Verifier: sweeps the full 2^N input space, validates functionality against courseware specs, and generates an HMAC-SHA256 sealed A4 PDF certificate.

SENSORY & CUSTOMIZATION
• 10 Hardware Themes: Obsidian Stealth, Amber CRT, HP Slate, Cleanroom White, Tektronix Blue, Cyberpunk Neon, and more.
• Synthesized Audio & Haptics: mechanical switch clicks, relay snaps, and tactile feedback generated via AudioTrack without external audio files.
