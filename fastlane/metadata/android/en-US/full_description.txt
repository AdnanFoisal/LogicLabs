Logic Labs is a fully offline digital-logic laboratory: a digital twin of the K&H IDL-800A Digital Lab Trainer, with a 2.5D breadboard, datasheet-exact 74xx TTL chips and a discrete-event simulation engine derived from hneemann/Digital.

THE BREADBOARD
• AD-200 topology: 1,896 tie-points, four 64-column terminal blocks, seven distribution rails and the DIP centre trench
• Wire by hand: drag or tap-to-connect jumpers with magnetic snapping, a 2.2× magnifier loupe, haptic feedback and ten insulation colours
• 14 datasheet-exact 74xx parts: 7400, 7402, 7404, 7408, 7410, 7411, 7420, 7432, 74266, 7483, 7486, 7474, 7476, 7448
• In-app IC reference with pinouts for every chip

THE CONSOLE
• 8 logic switches (SW0–SW7), 2 debounced pulsers and 8 buffered LED monitors with afterglow
• Detented clock generator from 0.5 Hz to 100 kHz
• Dual BCD-driven seven-segment displays and a master power switch
• Optional PRACTICAL mode: reversed polarity and bus contention can destroy chips, like on a real bench

INSTRUMENTS
• Dual-channel oscilloscope: probe board and console signals, CH1 / CH2 / DUAL view, adjustable timebase and volts per division
• Boolean diagram view: the gate-level schematic of your build, with live net colouring and derived boolean expressions per output

COURSEWORK & VERIFICATION
• A classic university lab curriculum plus extended experiments: universal NAND/NOR builds, boolean simplification, adders and subtractors, code converters, decoders, comparators, multiplexers/demultiplexers, latches, flip-flops, ripple counters, discrete diode/RTL gates and a weighted-resistor D/A converter, each with objective checklists
• One-tap truth-table verification sweeps the full input space; results carry an HMAC-SHA256 hash and can be exported as an A4 PDF certificate

THEMES & FEEDBACK
• Nine hardware themes (including Obsidian, Amber CRT, HP Slate, Cleanroom, Cyberpunk Neon, Tokyo Night, Solarized Dark, Vintage British Lab and Titanium Frost), or follow the system light/dark setting
• Synthesized console sounds and haptics, individually toggleable — no bundled audio files

100% OFFLINE
No network permission. No accounts. No tracking. No ads. Projects are saved on your device with autosave and JSON import/export.

The simulation kernel is derived from the GPLv3 project hneemann/Digital, which sets the license for the whole app (GPL-3.0-or-later).
